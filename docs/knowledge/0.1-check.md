# Knowledge check — Module 0.1

Closed book. No runbooks, ADRs, notes or chat history open. Write your answers
on paper or in a scratch file, then grade against `0.1-answer-key.md`.

**Pass bar:** teach-back covers at least 8 of the 10 required elements; at least
10 of 12 concept questions fully correct; **every** failure scenario names a safe
first action and the correct most-likely cause. Below the bar, the module stays
open.

---

## Part 1: Teach-back

On a blank page, reproduce Module 0.1's build in order, from an unknown server to
a hardened, inventoried one. For each major step write what you do **and why it
comes at that point in the order**. Include:

- how the workstation reaches the iDRAC and why it's isolated;
- the evidence you capture first and what it's for;
- the account change and the order of the steps that make it safe;
- which iDRAC services are left on and which are off, with one reason each;
- the BIOS settings that make the serial console work, and how you prove it
  before installing an OS;
- how you map disks to physical bays on this server;
- what you check about memory and when you're allowed to read it;
- the power redundancy setting and what it does;
- how the configuration export is produced and checked.

Then rebuild one piece from your page alone: on the live system, read back the
BIOS serial settings and the iDRAC protocol settings and confirm they match what
you wrote, without opening the runbook.

## Part 2: Concept questions

1. Why is the H330 run in HBA mode under mdadm instead of one RAID0 virtual disk
   per drive? Name two concrete problems the RAID0 approach would create.
2. You run `racadm set BIOS.SysSecurity.AcPwrRcvry Last` and `racadm get` still
   shows the old value. What happened, what makes the change take effect, and
   why can't you stage two separate BIOS jobs?
3. What does `SerialComm` decide, and what does `SerialPortAddress` decide? Why
   was `console com2` black when every value looked right?
4. Why are the iDRAC's NTP and future syslog targets configured by IP address,
   and why is the iDRAC deliberately **not** joined to FreeIPA?
5. Why is IPMI over LAN disabled, and why does nmap still report UDP 623 as open?
   How do you prove IPMI is really off?
6. Explain nmap's `open`, `closed`, `filtered` and `open|filtered`. Why are UDP
   results harder to trust than TCP results?
7. A4 and A8 were missing. What two explanations fit, what test separates them,
   and which result would have meant the CPU channel was at fault?
8. Why is a multi-bit memory error a bigger threat to data on mdadm RAID5 than a
   single failed disk?
9. During the build, `jobqueue create … -r pwrcycle` was correct. Why is it wrong
   once RHEL is installed, and what do you do instead?
10. Before disabling root you scrambled its password and ran `ssh -O exit`. What
    does each step protect against?
11. Why is the ESP mirror built with mdadm metadata 1.0, and why does
    `efibootmgr` need two RHEL entries?
12. What does "Input Power Redundant" guarantee on this server, and what does it
    not protect against?

## Part 3: Failure-mode scenarios

For each: most likely cause(s), your **first safe action**, then the fix.

**A.** From the admin VM, the iDRAC doesn't answer ping or SSH.
`ip neigh` shows `FAILED`. Link LEDs are green.

**B.** After a clean reboot, `console com2` connects but stays black for two
minutes. The host is reachable over SSH.

**C.** The storage log shows `PDR87` (reset) twice, then `PDR3` (not functioning),
then `PDR5` (removed) for `Disk.Bay.2`. The RAID5 is now degraded. You need to
find the physical caddy.

**D.** Straight after `serveraction powerup`, `hwinventory` lists fewer DIMMs than are installed and the
disk list is empty. Five minutes later the disks are back.

**E.** You exported the configuration profile and want to commit it to the repo.
What must you check first, and how, before it leaves the admin VM?
